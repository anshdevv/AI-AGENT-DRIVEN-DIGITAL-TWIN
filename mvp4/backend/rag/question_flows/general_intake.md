# Question Flow: General Intake

Ask these questions one at a time:

1. What is the main problem you want the doctor to review today?
2. When did it start?
3. Is it getting better, worse, or staying the same?
4. How severe is it right now on a scale of 1 to 10?
5. Do you have fever, vomiting, bleeding, or trouble breathing with it?
6. Do you have any major medical condition or are you pregnant?
7. Have you taken any medicine or received any treatment for this already?
8. Is there anything else the doctor should know before the visit?

Red Flags:
- Chest pain
- Difficulty breathing
- Severe bleeding
- Fainting
- Sudden confusion

Rules:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Keep the wording short and neutral.
- Do not diagnose.
- Stop and escalate if the patient reports a red flag.
